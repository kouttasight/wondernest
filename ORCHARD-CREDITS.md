# WonderNest · The Orchard Picnic

This is a real-time 3D browser-game prototype, with no clinical-effectiveness claim.

## Nori’s animated fox base

Fox model by PixelMannen, CC0. Rigging and animations by tomkranis, CC BY 4.0. Conversion to glTF by @AsoboStudio and @scurest, CC BY 4.0. Distributed through Khronos glTF Sample Assets:
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modified for WonderNest with Loop subdivision, an AI-enhanced fur albedo texture, resizing, rendering/material changes, a mint scarf, gold star and a carried-object attachment. The original idle and walk animations are retained. Full source asset license is in orchard-fox-license.md.

## Other 3D artwork

Mazidah’s procedural rabbit, fur, bow, garden geometry, trees, leaves, apples, book tiles and other game objects were authored for this prototype. These are approximations of natural forms, rather than scanned animals.

## Music and voice
