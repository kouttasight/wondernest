Sound & comfort

Music lowers under speech and pauses during menus and breaks.

Still poses, a steady wide camera and direct travel when tapping or using HELP. The arrows remain under your control.

No timers, scores, microphone, required speech or failure screens. Settings and learning choices pause the world. Picture-choice settings apply when a new adventure starts.

About this 3D prototype & credits

Real-time 3D animals and scenery, photographic terrain textures and environmental lighting. Nori uses a licensed rigged fox model; Mazidah is a procedural fur-covered rabbit. This is a play prototype with no clinical-effectiveness claim.
