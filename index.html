Sound & movement

Pre-recorded synthetic speech with a playful, youthful guide voice. Short prompts name actions, numbers, colours, shapes and letters. No device robot voice or electronic beeps.

Sound starts after your first tap. STOP, BREAK, options, the map and video choices silence the guide. New prompts replace earlier ones. Pictures remain usable with sound off.

Reduced motion keeps poses still and moves Nori directly to tapped destinations. Arrow movement remains under your control.

How much to explore

Task changes start a fresh version of the current adventure. Other adventures start fresh with those choices when next opened. Word play is optional supported copying with a visible model.

Play together

Tap an object to make Nori walk over and use it. Arrows also move Nori. The big action button says Go when a destination is farther away, then changes to Pick, Give, Plant, Water or the next action when close. HELP brings Nori to a useful place without doing the action.

MORE opens the map; AGAIN restarts this adventure. A finished chapter offers Next chapter. AGAIN starts another round with another colour, shape, letter or word. Story and chapter choices are always available. There is no target time, score, failure screen or required speech. Take breaks whenever wanted.

Keyboard: arrows to move, Space to jump. All buttons also support Tab and Enter/Space. No dragging is required.
